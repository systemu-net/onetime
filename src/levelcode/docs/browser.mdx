# LevelCode in your browser

> The LevelCode workbench in a browser tab. Sign in with your account, open a folder or a scratch workspace, and work with the chat panel and the agent. Nothing to install.

The browser edition is the real LevelCode workbench running in a tab. The explorer, editor tabs, themes, the chat panel and the agent are all there. You sign in with the same LevelCode account as the Mac app, and the gateway plan and usage are the same.

A few things stay in the Mac app. The browser edition has no terminal, and the agent does not run shell commands. The [full list](#what-stays-in-the-mac-app) is below.

## Open it

Open [LevelCode in your browser](/web) and choose **Open LevelCode**. If you are signed in, **Open in browser** on your [account page](/account) goes to the same place.

The editor opens in the same tab.

## Sign in

The browser edition uses your LevelCode account, the same one you use in the Mac app. The gateway plan and usage are the same as in the Mac app.

Sign-in happens in a separate tab. If the sign-in tab does not close, go back to the editor tab.

If the sign-in tab does not open at all, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

## Where your files live

There are two ways to work with files.

### Open a folder from your computer

LevelCode uses the File System Access API, so edits go straight to the folder you picked. This works in Chrome and Edge.

### Use a scratch workspace

A scratch workspace is saved in your browser. It works in every modern browser, including Safari and Firefox.

<Note>
  Files stay in your browser or on your computer. [Privacy](#privacy) covers what goes to the model.
</Note>

## What the agent can do here

The agent can read, search, create, edit and delete files in the open workspace.

It cannot run shell commands, and there is no terminal. Work that needs either one stays in the Mac app.

## What stays in the Mac app

These are not in the browser edition.

- The terminal
- Running shell commands
- MCP servers that start local processes
- Installing extensions

They stay in the Mac app. Get it from the [download page](/download), or see [Install and setup](/docs/setup).

## Privacy

Files stay in your browser or on your computer.

The text you send in chat and the files you ask LevelCode to read go to the model through the LevelCode gateway, the same as in the Mac app. [Cloud and billing](/docs/cloud#privacy) explains how requests are routed on the gateway.
